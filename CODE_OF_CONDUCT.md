# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is shortened for a small project.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with newcomers, and welcoming different views and experience.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), marked as a conduct report, or by opening an issue that asks for a private contact without giving details. Reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers who are the subject of a report will not take part in handling it.
